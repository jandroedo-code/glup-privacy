Data used by Glup

Glup stores the amounts and dates you log, drink types, your daily goal, fish name and aquarium selection, the start of fish care, favorite amounts, reminder settings and optional connection preferences on your device. Older entries without a drink type are treated as water.

Purchases and Glup Pro access are verified through Apple’s StoreKit. The app stores the access status needed to unlock features. Glup does not receive your payment card details.

Purpose and choices

These data provide the journal, drink statistics, aquarium, widgets and reminders you request. All drinks count by their logged volume toward your Glup goal, without hydration multipliers.

The original fish and basic logging are free. iCloud, Apple Health and notifications are optional and require your choice or system authorization. You can use, export and delete your journal without enabling these connections or subscribing to Pro.

Glup Pro and purchases

Glup Pro is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription managed by Apple. It includes widgets, visual history and drink filters, individual corrections, favorite amounts, adaptive reminders and access to all species while the subscription is active.

The configured base price for Spain is €1.99 per month. Other regions display the applicable price in their local currency. Eligible accounts may receive a seven-day free trial; availability depends on the offer terms and eligibility determined by Apple. Before confirmation, the app and Apple’s purchase sheet show the applicable terms and subsequent price.

After the trial, the subscription renews at the stated price unless you cancel. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Billing and renewal terms are displayed in Apple’s confirmation.

Fish purchased individually are separate, non-consumable one-time purchases and remain owned after the subscription ends. Access to species included only with Pro ends when that access expires; the original fish remains available. Uninstalling the app or deleting the journal does not cancel a subscription.

Local storage and tracking

Glup has no separate user account, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated server receiving your journal or fish name. It does not sell these data or use them for advertising or tracking across apps.

Apple may process purchases, App Store information, backups or diagnostics according to your Apple settings and its privacy policy.

iCloud

If enabled, the journal — including amounts, dates, drink types and deletion markers — and daily goal synchronize with the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Fish names and aquarium preferences are stored locally.

Turning iCloud off stops future synchronization but does not erase existing iCloud data. Apple operates this service under its terms and privacy policy. Backups and iCloud data are also managed through Apple’s settings.

Apple Health

If you enable the connection and authorize access, Glup writes new water and sparkling water entries to Water in Apple Health. Coffee, tea, milk and other drinks remain in Glup’s journal and are not written as water in Health. No caffeine or other nutrition permissions are requested.

Glup does not request access to read Health data from other apps or automatically send old journal entries. To correct samples it created, Glup may look up its own samples by identifier. Amount or date corrections update its own sample; reclassifying water as another drink or deleting an entry individually removes its own water sample when the change is processed. Glup does not modify samples from other apps.

Disabling the connection pauses pending and new exports until you enable it again. Pending changes remain on the device for retry when the connection and authorization are available. Deleting the whole journal preserves entries already sent to Health; manage those in the Health app. These data are not used for advertising.

Widgets

Widgets share the journal and display data — goal, fish name and species, favorite amounts and Pro access status — through a private on-device App Group. They use a copy of app-verified Pro access until its expiry; opening Glup refreshes renewals, restorations and revocations.

Widget buttons log water. Entries are saved locally, and optional Health and iCloud exports are processed when Glup opens or when the action runs in the app’s process. iOS manages visual widget updates.

Notifications and fish names

Reminders are scheduled locally through the operating system. Their text includes the exact fish name you saved, or “your fish” if no name is set. They may appear on the Lock Screen depending on your notification preview settings.

Change the name, schedule or sound in Glup, or disable notifications in system settings. Adaptive Pro reminders use your entries and pauses to schedule up to 48 hours ahead; opening Glup or logging from a widget refreshes the schedule. No developer-operated server sends push notifications.

Retention, export and deletion

The journal remains until you delete it. Export a CSV containing identifiers, dates, amounts and drink types or delete the whole journal in Privacy and data without Pro. Pro also allows individual corrections and deletions.

Deletions retain minimal markers, including the identifier and dates needed for synchronization, to prevent an older device from restoring removed entries. If iCloud is enabled, changes synchronize when connected. Fish preferences remain.

Deleting the journal does not remove purchases, cancel Pro or erase entries already sent to Health. Turning iCloud off does not itself erase existing iCloud data. Backups and existing iCloud data are also managed through Apple.

Support inquiries

If you contact support by email, we use your address and the content you send to respond to your inquiry. Do not include passwords or health information that is not needed to explain the issue.

Your rights

Access and export your journal, delete records and withdraw optional permissions. Pro allows individual corrections. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to complain to the competent data protection authority.

For questions about your data, contact infostudiovertigo@gmail.com. The publisher cannot retrieve a journal stored only on your device or access your private iCloud database through the app as its developer.

Changes to this policy

The date on this page identifies this policy version. We will update it if Glup’s data practices change. New optional data access will require the relevant choice or system authorization.
