Data used by Glup

Glup stores the water amounts and dates you enter, your daily goal, your fish name and aquarium selection, reminder settings and optional connection preferences on your device. Purchase entitlements are verified through Apple's StoreKit. Glup does not receive your payment card details.

Purpose and choices

These data provide the water journal, aquarium and reminders you request. Core records are used to provide the app's functionality. iCloud, Apple Health and notifications are optional and require your choice or system authorization. You can use the journal without enabling them or buying fish.

Local storage and tracking

This version has no Glup account, advertising, analytics SDKs or developer-operated server receiving your water journal or fish name. It does not sell your data or use it for advertising. Apple may process App Store, device backup or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and its own privacy policy.

iCloud

If enabled, water records and the daily goal are synchronized with the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Fish names and aquarium preferences are currently stored locally. Turning iCloud off stops future synchronization; it does not erase existing iCloud data. Apple operates this service under its terms and privacy policy, including its applicable data transfer safeguards.

Apple Health

If enabled and authorized, Glup writes new water entries to Apple Health. It does not request access to read other health data or automatically send previous entries. These data are not used for advertising. Disabling the connection stops future writes. Entries already saved in Health must be managed in the Health app.

Notifications and fish names

Reminders are scheduled locally through the operating system. Their text includes the exact fish name you saved, or ‘your fish’ if no name is set. The text may appear on your lock screen depending on your notification preview settings. You can change the name, schedule or sound in Glup and disable notifications in the system settings.

Retention and deletion

Water records remain until you delete them. Export a CSV or delete your journal in Privacy and data. Deleting the journal clears its water amounts and replaces them with minimal deletion markers so synchronization does not restore deleted entries. If iCloud is enabled, these deletions are synchronized when connected. Fish preferences remain. Deleting the journal does not remove existing Health entries or App Store purchases. Device backups and existing iCloud data are managed through Apple's settings.

Your rights

You can access your journal in Glup, export it, remove incorrect records by deleting the journal and entering new records, and withdraw optional permissions at any time. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. The publisher cannot retrieve a journal stored only on your device.

Changes to this policy

The date above identifies this version. This policy will be updated if Glup's data practices change. New optional data access will require the relevant choice or system permission.
